Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version

  • Pogo has no sign-up, no passwords and no chat. We (Harvizl Studios) do not run servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress (coins, upgrades, outfits and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The online leaderboard shows a random nickname (like “SwiftOtter#4821”) and your best height to other players. It never shows your real name. Tap HIDE ME on the leaderboard to hide yourself.
  • Pogo is free because it shows ads. Our ad partner, Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads), collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • You choose whether ads can be personalised. Change it any time in Settings › AD CHOICES. You see the same number of ads either way.

Who we are

Pogo is a mobile game for Android and iOS made by Harvizl Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play Pogo, who handles it, and the choices you have. It applies only to the Pogo app.

What Pogo saves on your device

To remember your game, Pogo saves the following in the app’s own storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Coins, best height, upgrades, power-ups, outfits and trails you own and have equipped
  • Sound and music volume
  • Your answer to the ad choice question
  • A sign-in token for the online leaderboard (see below)

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling Pogo deletes it, apart from your leaderboard entry, which stays online until you remove it.

Online leaderboard

Pogo has a worldwide leaderboard of the 1,000 highest jumps. It is run by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards), operated by Unity Technologies. To take part, Pogo signs you in anonymously in the background. There is no sign-up and you never enter an email address, name or password.

Nicknames are generated automatically and players cannot type their own, so the leaderboard never shows real names or messages.

To hide yourself, open the leaderboard and tap HIDE ME, then confirm. Your nickname and best height are then no longer shown to anyone, and Pogo stops sending scores. Your entry stays stored on Unity Gaming Services, out of view, so that JOIN can put you back later with the same nickname and best. To have your leaderboard entry and anonymous player ID deleted completely, contact us.

If Unity has to act on a report about a player account, for example under the EU Digital Services Act, Pogo shows you Unity’s notice in the game.

Ads in Pogo

Pogo shows two kinds of video ads:

  • Optional reward ads. After a run you can choose to watch one to double your coins. Nothing happens unless you tap to watch.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between runs.

Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, and the Unity Ads network, both operated by Unity Technologies and its affiliates. To show, measure and protect ads, these services collect information from your device directly. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Your ad choices

The first time you open Pogo, we ask whether our ad partners may use information from your device to show you more relevant ads.

  • Allow: ads may be personalised using your advertising ID and ad activity.
  • Don’t Allow: ads are not personalised, and we tell Unity not to sell or share your information for targeted advertising. You still see ads, chosen without your personal profile.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings › AD CHOICES. You can also:

  • On iPhone and iPad: Pogo asks for permission to track only if you chose Allow. You can turn tracking off later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact menu varies by device).

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent.

  • UK and EEA: personalised ads are shown only with your consent, which you can withdraw in AD CHOICES. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • US states with privacy laws (including California): choosing Don’t Allow opts you out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

You can hide your leaderboard entry yourself at any time with HIDE ME, and ask us to delete your entry and anonymous player ID completely. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about ad or leaderboard data are best sent to Unity using the options in its policy linked above. You are also welcome to contact us and we will help.

Children

Pogo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s information has been collected through Pogo, contact us and we will work with our ad partner to have it deleted.

How long information is kept

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall Pogo. Your leaderboard entry stays online, shown or hidden as you choose, until you ask us to delete it. Unity keeps ad and service data for the periods described in its privacy policy. Unity may process this information in countries other than your own, using the safeguards described in its policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Pogo handles information, for example by adding a new ad partner, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.
